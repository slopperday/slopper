## Platform

web

## Users

People who build with AI (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) and want the habit of shipping: indie makers, side-project
people, students, anyone who has more half-built ideas than launches. They come back daily, mostly on a laptop while
shipping and on a phone while showing things to people or checking votes (inferred).

## Product Purpose

Slopper pushes people to ship something live every day. You set a goal (30 slops in 30 days), launch daily, show each
launch to people, and keep slopping until one is slopcessful. Success is a maker who keeps shipping past the point they
would have quit, and the one launch in thirty that breaks out.

## Positioning

Volume over taste: Slopper celebrates the daily attempt, not the polished product. The goal grid of shipped days, the
daily race that closes at midnight UTC, and the hall of fame for slops that broke out are built around repetition and
persistence, where launch sites are built around one big day.

## Operating Context

- Goals run in the maker's own time zone; a day ends at midnight where they are.
- Slop of the day is a race on votes for each UTC day, crowned just after midnight UTC.
- Each launch (website, then iOS beta, then 2.0) is its own entry with its own shot at slop of the day.
- Updates, new features, re-promotes and field tests keep a slop alive after launch day.
- Agents can do everything through MCP (`/mcp`) and REST (`/api/v1`), with copy-paste prompts on every form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Goals and streaks, the daily race, the wall (all slops, by category, freshest or sticking), the Slopcessful hall of
fame, launches and relaunches, updates and field tests, upvotes with private downvotes, feedback and kudos, "take my
money" pledges (non-binding, no money moves), a per-product money ledger (private unless shared), auto-posting to X,
LinkedIn and Bluesky, live notifications, flags and a moderator queue, API tokens. Everything updates live.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Slopper, at slopper.day. Tagline: "Throw slop at the wall. Every day."
- Voice: plain words, cheeky. Throw slop at the wall, keep slopping until it's slopcessful. No kitchen, cooking,
  restaurant or food imagery or wording, ever.
- Logo: the "Drip Bang" mark, a fresh splat dripping into an exclamation mark that trails the wordmark, so the lockup
  reads "slopper!".
- Owner's taste: simple, visual, distinctive, engaging; little twists on familiar things; never a clone.

## Evidence on Hand

Real data only: no testimonials, user counts, revenue or press exist yet. Pledges are signals of interest, never
payments or investments, and the copy must not say otherwise.

## Product Principles

1. Shipping today beats shipping well.
2. Show the streak: the shape of your days is the product.
3. Celebrate attempts publicly; keep criticism (downvotes) private.
4. Safe for work, always: nothing invites NSFW content.
5. Agents are first-class users.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark, keyboard and screen-reader support, 44px touch targets, reduced motion respected,
no horizontal overflow at 375px.
