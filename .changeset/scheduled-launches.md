# Schedule launches for later

Got three things ready at once? Throw one now and line the rest up. Pick "Next free day" and each goes out on the next day with nothing out or scheduled, right as that day's race starts, so it gets all 24 hours of votes. Or pick a day and time yourself. Nobody sees a scheduled slop until it goes out; then it counts towards your goal on that day and auto-posts if you asked. Your goal grid shows the days with something coming, and the new slop page lists what's waiting, with buttons to throw one now or unschedule it.
