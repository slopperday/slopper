# Slop of the day counts only fair votes

Every vote still shows on a slop, whenever it's cast. The daily race now counts only the votes cast on its own day (votes in the minute after midnight used to slip in), and leaves out votes from accounts less than a day old, downvotes from makers racing the same day, and upvotes two racing makers swap. The rules page says what counts.
