# A moderation team, and videos that skip the queue

Slopper has a moderation team now. Moderators approve or reject promo videos and flagged slops; admins do that too, and run the team from the moderation page.

Once a moderator approves one of your promo videos, you're trusted: your next videos are checked automatically and play straight away when they pass. A video the check doesn't pass, or can't check, waits for a moderator as before.
