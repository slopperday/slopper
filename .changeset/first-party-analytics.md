# Slopper keeps its own analytics

Slopper now records how it's used (pages opened, clicks, where visits come from) to learn what works and what's broken. It's first-party: it stays in Slopper's own database and no analytics company sees it. Turn it off in any browser from Settings or the privacy page, which now explains it all.
