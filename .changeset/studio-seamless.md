# Post to every account without surprises

- A checklist for each account says whether it's ready ("Instagram needs a picture or a video") with a button that takes you to the fix.
- Give a network or one account its own link, pictures and first comment, and go back to the shared version in one click.
- Send at the same time everywhere or pick a time per account. Times are read in your profile's time zone, across clock changes, and the composer says which.
- "Last used" and "Post as <product>" pick a set of accounts in one tap.
- Save ideas before choosing any accounts. What you're writing is kept in your browser and offered back if you leave.
- Two X accounts can't post the same words (X's rules), and each X post shows what X will bill your app.
- A post that may or may not have gone out is never sent again on its own: check the network, then tell Slopper it posted or send it again.
- Settings → Accounts shows whether each account is connected, expiring soon or needs reconnecting, and a step-by-step guide for setting up every network.
- Reddit connects with your Reddit account, and each subreddit you add is its own destination. LinkedIn Company Pages can be connected once LinkedIn approves the app.
- Best-time suggestions are per account and labelled as estimates; previews are labelled as approximations.
