# The studio: write one post and send it to all your accounts at once

Write once in the studio and post it to X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Threads, Facebook Pages, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube,
as whichever brand it's for. Each network gets a copy that fits it, and you see how it will look before it goes out.

- Threads, a first comment, alt text, LinkedIn PDF documents, video covers and YouTube thumbnails.
- Drafts, editing, a weekly posting queue, the best times to post from your own results, repeating posts, staggered
  accounts, a pause button and bulk scheduling from a spreadsheet.
- A calendar with drag to reschedule, filters, an Instagram grid and a feed for your calendar app.
- Preview links that a client or teammate opens without an account to approve or ask for changes.
- Snippets (hashtag groups, signatures, templates, saved replies), variables and spintax, and a writing assistant that
  follows each brand's voice.
- Comments and replies, taking posts down, reposts by your other accounts, an auto-plug reply once a post takes off,
  and alerts when an account needs reconnecting.
- Analytics by network, account, format and hashtag, tracked links that count clicks per network, and a CSV export.
- Posts from RSS feeds, and signed webhooks for Zapier, Make or your own code.
