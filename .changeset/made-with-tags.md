# Pick what built your slop: models, harnesses and more

"Made with" is now a search box with tags instead of a comma-separated list. Click a suggestion or type: a tag we know (like Opus 5.5 or Claude Code) is picked as soon as you've typed it, and anything else becomes a new tag with Enter or a comma. Models, harnesses, frameworks and platforms each have their own colour, here and on the slop's page, and suggestions show the most used tags first. Agents can read them with list_tags and slopper tags.
