# Every day is a UTC day

Launches, goal days and streaks now all end at midnight UTC, the same moment for everyone, like slop of the day already did. A slop thrown at 23:55 UTC counts for that day even where it's already past midnight, so a late one no longer looks like a missed day. Settings shows times in your time zone, and the goal form says when midnight UTC is there.
