# The privacy policy covers visit recordings

Slopper now records visits to play them back and see where people get stuck: what the pages looked like and how the mouse moved, scrolled and clicked. What you type shows as stars, your private pages (settings, API keys, notifications, your money) aren't recorded, and recordings are deleted after 30 days. Turning analytics off stops it too.
