# Edits close after launch day

A launch can be edited until the end of its launch day, or for 12 hours after it goes out if that's later. Then the original post is locked, so what people voted on stays what they saw, and news goes in updates, which stay open. Its maker sees when editing closes on the slop page and the edit page. The promo video isn't locked: a moderator approves each one anyway.
