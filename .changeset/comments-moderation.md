# Comments are back, checked and moderated

Every slop page takes feedback and kudos again. A new comment passes the content check: blocked words are refused, and anything mature or unchecked waits for a moderator before it appears. People can flag comments, and enough flags hide one until a moderator decides. Approve someone's comment once and they are trusted: their later comments skip the check and the hourly cap.
