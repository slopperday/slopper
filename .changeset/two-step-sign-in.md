# Two-step sign-in with passkeys or an authenticator app

Turn it on in Settings → Security. Add a passkey (your fingerprint, face, device PIN or a security key) or an
authenticator app, and signing in asks for it after GitHub, Google, X, LinkedIn or Facebook, so a stolen login for one
of those isn't enough to get into your account and every brand it posts as. You get ten recovery codes in case you
lose your phone. Making API tokens, approving the CLI, connecting accounts, and setting up webhooks and feeds ask you to
confirm it's you again after 15 minutes. The security page also lists where you're signed in, signs out other
browsers, and keeps a log of sign-ins and changes.
