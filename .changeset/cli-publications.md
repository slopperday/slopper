# Launch a PDF or EPUB you wrote from the CLI

`--publication ./guide.pdf` (or `./zine.epub`) on `slopper launch` uploads a book, zine, guide or paper (up to 4 MB) to read from the slop page, and makes `--link` optional. On `slopper edit` it swaps the file, and `--remove-publication` takes it off.
