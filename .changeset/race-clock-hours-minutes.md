# The race clock reads hours and minutes all day

The countdown to midnight UTC used to switch to minutes and seconds in the last hour, so 58 minutes left read 58:45. It now reads hours and minutes all day (00:58 in the last hour, still in red) and never shows 00:00 while the race is open. Hover over it, or press and hold on a phone, to see the seconds too.
