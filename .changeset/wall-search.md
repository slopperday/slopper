# Search the wall, and pick several categories at once

The wall's categories are chips now: pick as many as you like, turn one off by clicking it again, or select all and clear them in one go. A search box sits above them and finds slops by what they do as well as by their words, so “budget” turns up an expense tracker. Search and chips work together, and both live in the address, so a filtered view can be shared and Back steps through it.
