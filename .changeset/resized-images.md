# Screenshots and profile pictures load at the size they're shown

Covers on the wall, the home page and profiles, screenshots on a slop's page, update images and profile pictures now download resized for the screen, as WebP or AVIF. The wall used to fetch about 4 MB of full-size screenshots for its small covers; now it fetches a small fraction of that.
