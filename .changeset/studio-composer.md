# A simpler, friendlier Studio

Writing a post now happens in one card: who it's from at the top, then the words, pictures, link and first comment, with a tab for each network when you want to say something differently there. New posts start with the accounts you usually use, and you can save the way you post as a workflow (the accounts, now or the queue, what it's about) and start every new post with it. Posts don't need to be about one of your slops: About starts at Anything. Drop or paste pictures straight in, press ⌘/Ctrl+Enter to send, and the checklist beside the preview says what each account needs, with Fix and Leave it out.
